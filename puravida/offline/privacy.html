Privacy Policy for Pura Vida Offline Backgammon

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Applies to the paid offline Android edition, com.aiassistlabs.puravida.offline.

The short version

Pura Vida Offline Backgammon, provided by AI ASSIST LLC, lets you play against the computer or another person on the same device. It needs no app account or sign-in. Your settings, score and saved game stay on your device. The app does not send your gameplay or settings to us or a game server. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following in its private Android SharedPreferences storage:

  • Your settings, including sound, haptics, hints, difficulty, match length, move input and display preferences
  • Your local score and playing record against the computer and in games on the same device
  • Your saved game, including the board position, turn, doubling cube and any saved dice, so you can continue
  • Small local flags recording whether you have made a move and which app version's notices you have seen

These values remain until replaced or removed by the app, or until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's local settings, score and saved game.

The app is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. It also opts out of Android WebView usage metrics.

What leaves your device

The offline release is configured without internet permission and with online play disabled. Its game, fonts and other assets are bundled with the app. During offline play, the app does not transmit personal data, settings, scores or saved games off your device. We do not collect, sell or share that data.

If you choose to email support or open an external website, your email app or browser handles that action. Messages you choose to send leave your device through that service. The game does not attach your local settings, scores or saved game.

Purchases

Google Play handles the purchase of this paid app under Google's terms and privacy practices. The app does not ask for or receive your payment card details. There are no in-app purchases.

Security

The offline app does not request internet permission and makes no network connections of its own. Links you choose to open are handled by your browser or email app.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over.

The app collects no personal data from any user, including children. For questions about children and privacy, email support@aiassistlabs.com.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's data practices change.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to AI ASSIST LLC at support@aiassistlabs.com. If you contact us, we receive the email address and message you choose to send.
